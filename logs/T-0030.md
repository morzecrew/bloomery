## Plan

P1 is a command shaped as a gate, counts of the surfaces actually checked, and
the documentation that makes the exit code a CI contract. It is smaller than §2
describes, because `resolve` has since gained the exit-code contract §2 says it
lacks and `SpecEncoder` has since gained the refusal serialisation D6 asks for.
§4 is the rejected alternative and is emphatic: no importer, no manifest, no dbt.
