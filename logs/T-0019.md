# T-0019 · RFC 0038 — Measure semantic types and additivity algebra

Executed on branch `feat/measure-semantic-types`. §12's first phase, read against the tree
rather than against the RFC's account of it: the vocabulary D5 predicted is already
scattered across three places, and what is actually missing is the check that an
`additivity: additive` claim is true.

**Drift count: 0.**

Where building this disagreed with the design for it, written at the moment it happened.
Nothing here is revised afterwards to agree with what was later settled, and nothing here
has been folded back into the RFC's text. Rows proposed below are put forward for the
author to accept or refuse; execution does not write them into a decision table itself.

| Class | Test | Meaning |

## D-102 — the closed set widens the enum, not the authored keyword

```divergence
decision: RFC 0038 D1 — the aggregation vocabulary is a closed typed set
grade: UNLISTED
class: spec-gap
at: 2026-09-05T09:45:00Z
attempt: 1
claim: D1 names six members and §12 requires the enum closed from the first commit, but it does not say whether authors write them; `Ratio` is today a decomposition block on a non_additive metric, so accepting `additivity: ratio` as well would give one fact two spellings and leave every guard asking which the author meant
evidence: src/bloomery/ir/nodes.py:755-760
action: decided
proposal: D1 gains a sentence — the closed set is the *resolved* vocabulary; authored `additivity:` keeps its three words and resolution maps them onto the six, per §7's "existing specifications resolve to the new semantic type where their current meaning is unambiguous". A member becomes authorable only when an author can say something with it that resolution cannot infer
```

- The reading matters beyond spelling: `Ratio` as an authored keyword would let a project
  declare `additivity: ratio` **without** a `ratio:` block, which is a metric claiming a
  decomposition it does not have — the exact shape `NonAdditiveWithoutComponents` exists to
  refuse.

## D-105 — widening the enum silently rewrites twenty branches that read it

```divergence
decision: unlisted — what the existing readers of `Additivity` do with the members D1 adds
grade: UNLISTED
class: discovery
at: 2026-09-05T10:05:00Z
attempt: 1
claim: twenty sites branch on `Additivity`, all of them on NON_ADDITIVE or SEMI_ADDITIVE, so three new members join every `is not Additivity.NON_ADDITIVE` as a silent catch-all that mypy cannot see; reading them shows they do not ask about additivity at all — they ask whether a metric is a stored measure or is computed at query time from components, which is `yes` for SNAPSHOT and DISTINCT_COUNT and `no` for RATIO
evidence: `grep -rn "Additivity\." src/bloomery/ | grep -v ir/nodes.py | wc -l` -> `20`
action: decided
proposal: D1 gains a sentence — closing the enum obliges the commit that closes it to leave no member silently reachable. Here that is met by §12's own staging: the three new members are defined and *not minted by resolution*, with a canary pinning exactly which members resolution can produce, so the commit that first mints one fails at that canary rather than inheriting twenty branches written for a different meaning
```

- The alternative — routing all twenty through a materializability predicate now — was rejected
  for this phase: it rewrites emitter and planner branches for members nothing can produce,
  and the rewrite would be verified by no test, since no fixture can reach it. The canary is
  the smaller thing that fails at the right moment.
- This is the third decision the RFC did not settle, after D-102 and D-103. All three were
  derivable from §4's per-class rollup rules and D1's own text rather than needing the author,
  which is why execution continued rather than stopping at the readiness gate — but three is
  the gate's threshold and the count is recorded here for the author to weigh.

## D-106 — the corpus's liveness check assumed an RFC ships all at once

```divergence
decision: unlisted — how a phased RFC, live but partly shipped, reads against the corpus outcome vocabulary
grade: UNLISTED
class: spec-gap
at: 2026-09-05T10:40:00Z
attempt: 1
claim: T-0018 built D3's stable-rule machinery as a biconditional — a case is unguarded if and only if its cited RFC is still live in rfcs/ — and that identity is false for an RFC that ships in phases; RFC 0038 D1 and D2 now refuse cases 002 and 005 while §12 stages DistinctCount and Snapshot lowering, so the RFC is correctly still live and the rule is correctly built, and the check fails on a tree where nothing is wrong
evidence: `uv run pytest tests/execution/test_semantic_corpus.py -q` -> `AssertionError: 002-average-of-averages/naive is refused and cites RFC 0038 D2, which is live`
action: decided
proposal: none for RFC 0042's text — D3 is satisfied by the rule ID being stable and machine-readable, which it is. The defect is in the check built for it, and the fix is to weaken the biconditional to the half that survives: a *retired* RFC may own no unguarded case, since retirement is the human act declaring the work complete. A live RFC implies nothing, because a phased one has shipped some of its rules and not others
```

- **What is kept.** §8's gate still fires in the direction that matters: retire RFC 0038 while
  a case still says `unguarded` on it, and the suite goes red saying the document was
  declared complete without converting a case it owns. That is the failure the number cannot
  report, and it is why the check exists at all.
- **What is dropped, and why it was never load-bearing.** The other direction asserted that a
  `refused` case cites a shipped rule. What makes a case refused is that bloomery refuses it,
  which `test_bloomery_does_what_the_case_says` asserts directly against the error class —
  so the dropped half was a proxy for something already proven, which is the same shape D-101
  removed from the `unguarded` side one branch earlier.
- The phenomenon was visible before this: RFC 0037 is ✅ Complete and deliberately still in
  `rfcs/` because its dependents argue in its vocabulary. No corpus case cited it, so the
  biconditional passed while already being false in general.

## D-107 — a derived origin grain cannot tell a snapshot key from a redundant one

```divergence
decision: RFC 0038 D6 — whether a measure's origin grain becomes authored syntax or stays derived from its entity
grade: OPEN
class: discovery
at: 2026-09-05T11:20:00Z
attempt: 2
claim: reading the origin grain off the entity key refuses two projects that are not snapshots — an entity keyed (payment_id, paid_at) is one row per payment, because paid_at is functionally dependent on payment_id, and the key is merely redundant; the refusal's own remediation is then a lie, since declaring semi_additive over paid_at would assert a rollup axis that does not exist. The distinguishing fact is not in the key at all: both false positives declare no mart, so the measure is never rolled up over anything, while case 005 declares a mart flattening as_of_day as a date dimension
evidence: `uv run pytest tests/unit/test_resolve/test_currency_convert.py::test_a_key_field_is_a_valid_anchor -q` -> `FalseAdditivityClaim: metric 'total_usd' ... 'paid_at' is part of that key`
action: decided
proposal: D6 closes as "derived, and gated on the axis being exposed" — the origin grain stays derived from the entity, and the additivity check fires only where a mart carrying the measure flattens the temporal determinant as a date dimension. That is §6's own rule ("rollup permission depends on both RFC 0037's grain proof and this RFC's aggregation semantics") rather than a new one, and it makes the message true whenever it is printed: the axis is declared, so the sum across it is what will happen. Authored origin grain is not needed for this phase and the question is not reopened by it
```

- **Found by the suite, not by the sweep.** The blast-radius measurement that preceded this
  read `tests/fixtures/` and reported zero, and it was wrong — three tests build their
  projects inline, where no fixture sweep could see them. A measurement's scope is part of
  its result, and "zero across 27 fixtures" was quoted as though it were "zero".
- The narrowing loses nothing the corpus asks for: case 005 declares the mart, which is what
  makes its wrong number reachable in the first place.
