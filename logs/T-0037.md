completion criterion and §9's first risk is a phase that adds nodes and stops.

and a corpus case; then per-measure restriction, the field, and whatever
replaces `_plannable`.

Out: target lowering from the plan (§4), `PreservingJoin` (RFC 0041 D10 keeps
it refused), `ConvertUnit` (§8, and its owner problem is not solved here), and
any widening of what is answerable — every shape here is answered today, so no
request may change outcome (D2).
