## Self-audit — 2026-09-10

Scope: the whole branch, 6 commits / 15 files / +1635-187 against `main`. Seven
findings, all fixed. Two in the code, five in what was checked.

**1 · An alias was flattened out of its metric.** An alias is scoped to the
metric that declares it (RFC 0034 D1), so two `derived:` metrics may both call
their offset input `prior` and mean different measures. `Offset.reads` keyed on
the alias alone and produced `Offset(prior = orders …, prior = revenue …)` — a
plan no target can lower, because the expressions above reference `prior` and
there is no longer one answer to which. Reproduced red, then the read gained
its owning metric. Not reachable through any current fixture, whose aliases
happen to differ; reachable through any project that writes two of these.

**2 · The `claims` test had become the list it warns about.**
`test_every_node_answers_whether_it_claims` enumerated the original five nodes
and went on passing while four more landed. Its own docstring says a membership
test over a closed vocabulary is right until the vocabulary gains a member —
and it was that test. D3 is `LOCKED` on this and §9 names it as the first thing
review should look for, and the close-out written above asserting all four
declare `claims = True` was written from a reading rather than from a test,
which is the verification failure pass 9 exists for.

It now reads the vocabulary off `PlanNode` and checks each kind against a
decided table, with a per-node assertion that a claiming node needs a closed
proof. The gate is against the table and not against `node.claims`: gating on
the node's own answer would let a node that lies about it skip its own test,
rebuilding the hole one level down. Verified by setting `Compute.claims` false
— red, and the file checksum-restored.

**3 · Four sabotage survivors, each a real gap.** Neither offset form was
asserted beyond `offset_window`, so a mutation ignoring `offset_to_grain` left
every suite green while the plan silently lost its shift — the metric still
planned, because a dropped read simply produced no node and the aggregate
beneath still carried its proof. `Offset`'s own sort, the scoped-filter guard
and the non-additive condition were untested. 12 of 12 after.

**4 · The patch-coverage number was measured against yesterday's file.** `just
coverage` writes a terminal report and no XML, so `coverage.xml` was 11 hours
stale and every patch number quoted for this branch described a different tree.
Regenerated with `coverage xml` from a fresh run.

**5 · What the fresh number then found, and sabotage had not.** Ten uncovered
added lines: the canonicalizing `__setattr__` in `Filter`, `Reduce` and
`Window` — the builders hand them sorted input, so those lines only run through
a direct construction, which is how a library caller reaches them; the empty
refusals on `Reduce` and `Offset`, where the `Compute` and `Window` equivalents
had tests and these two did not; `_frame`'s unreachable raise; and
`metric_restrictions`' unknown-name return. Each now has a test. The two
defensive branches are executed deliberately rather than called dead, which is
the separate claim pass 9 says needs its own evidence.

**6 · `guaranteed`'s premise, verified rather than asserted.**
`_measures_are_embedded`'s docstring says both its conditions are reachable
only where an earlier stage already refuses, and the two call sites rest on it.
Checked by running: a `non_additive` metric with neither a ratio nor a
`derived:` block is refused by the additivity guardrail before any mart carries
it, and a measure no mart carries is `UnreachableAtGrain` from the coverage
precheck.

**Verification.** `just quality -s` 9/9. 4404 passed, 5 skipped. Global
coverage 99.13% against a floor of 98. Patch coverage 100% (227/227) on the
full profile, from an XML written by the same run. Sabotage: twelve mutations
across `semantic/plan.py`, `planner/semantic_plan.py` and
`planner/metricflow_planner.py` — twelve killed, every file checksum-restored.

**Residue.** The two grouping helpers, `_semi_additive` and `_cumulative`, are
the same shape over different keys; extracting a generic grouper for two
callers would add indirection for less than it removes, so the repeat stands.
And §5.2's "it needs no new node either" is still wrong in the RFC — the
proposal is logged above and the amendment is the author's.
