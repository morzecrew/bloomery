# T-0055 · Exposure sinks and the additivity wording (RFC 0064 P3)

Executing RFC 0064 phase 3 (§12) against branch `feat/timeline-sinks`: the exposure sinks
from RFC 0056 on a `TimelineChange`, and the additivity facet "stated in RFC 0039's
provenance vocabulary". P3 is the last phase of the last document of the three-document
history sequence, so landing it retires 0064, and with it 0069 and 0062.

Nothing here is revised afterwards to agree with what was later settled, and nothing here

claim: §12's second P3 deliverable cannot be built as written. §5.4 asks the additivity facet to state a fact's movement in `Provenance` terms and gives the concrete case "a delta that moved a fact from DECLARED to INFERRED_HEURISTIC". No metric can produce that delta: additivity is read from the metric or its template and a metric silent in both fails to resolve, so every metric reaching a comparison has a DECLARED additivity and there is no second value to move to. The rewording would render a constant. §5.4's own *example* — a proven additive rollup becoming unproven — is a different and real question, but it is not the provenance of the additivity field: `prove_additive_rollup` takes a source and a target grain, which is a mart-level pair a metric-versus-metric diff does not have
evidence: src/bloomery/resolve/metrics.py:70-74
action: decided
proposal: ASSUMED — §5.4's additivity rewording is superseded, not built. The facet ships as D12 landed it, naming the field that moved and its before and after. The provenance sentence is unreachable for this facet and the rollup-provability reading is a different feature needing a rollup to ask about; recording that is worth more than a rendering that always says the same thing. RFC 0065 row 15 is the precedent: an RFC's worked example describing a refusal the compiler cannot produce, superseded rather than edited away
