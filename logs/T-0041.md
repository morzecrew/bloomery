## Plan

One member leaves one enum, and two tables keyed by its value lose a row. The
work is in showing that nothing needed it: the rule that names a composition is
reached from the shape of a derivation and never from a basis, and no code
builds a basis from a string.

## Self-audit — 2026-09-10

Scope: the whole branch against `main` — 1 commit at the time of the sweep, 9 files,
+180/-22, merge base `74174c5`. Every pass ran. Two findings, both from the sabotage
sweep and neither visible by re-reading.

1. **The claim the change rests on was untested.** Swapping `_determined_proof`'s
   composition branch from R005 to R007 killed nothing — so the argument for removing
   `TRANSITIVE` ("composition is recognised by a derivation having more than one step,
   and R005 names it") had no assertion behind it anywhere. It was reachable all along:
   the corpus holds 18 two-step derivations and 3 three-step ones. Closed with two tests
   over `coverage_check`, which reaches `customer.name` from the `order` grain by
   `many_to_one` then `entity_key` — one asserting the composition is R005 with a premise
   per hop, one asserting a single hop is *not* wrapped in a composition.
   Getting the first right needed the data rather than the reading: `Proof` sorts its
   premises at construction (D6, `LOCKED`), so the tuple is canonical and not
   chronological, and asserting the sequence would have been asserting the sort.
2. **The builder was named wrong in three places** — recorded above as a third `drift`.

**Verification.** `just test` 4214 passed / 5 skipped before the audit fixes;
`just quality -s` 9 of 9; `pyright src` clean; `just coverage` exit 0 at 99.12%; eight
hand-written mutants over the enum, both tables, the rule registry, both branches of the
composition and the evidence guard's abstention — one survived the first sweep and none
the second.

**Patch coverage is `n/a`, and that is the right answer.** The branch adds 180 lines and
**zero** of them are executable source: the source diff is a removal plus comments, so

on anything below `LOCKED`, so RFC 0065 P2's guard remains proved against its rule rather
than against a project.
