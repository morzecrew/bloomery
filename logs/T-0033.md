## Plan

P1 is the obligation as vocabulary: `prove_mart_rollup` and rule R013 under
`semantic/`, answering per measure whether a rollup keeping a given set of the
parent mart's dimensions may carry it. Row 13 fixes what P1 admits — `additive`,
and `ratio` over operands the rollup carries and P1 itself admits.

Out: the `rollup_of:`/`keep:` spec surface, the IR, resolution, the guardrail
that refuses (D5's wiring, as opposed to D5's answer), row 14's exclusion from
`measure_owners`, both derived-model emitters, Cube `pre_aggregations`, and
D6's corpus and execution-tier acceptance. Those are §12's P2 and P3.

One thing this phase deliberately does not do is reach the compile path. §9's
first risk is that the feature gets built early because it looks like an emitter
feature, and the mitigation is to build the invisible half first and where it
can be read. Nothing here is consulted by `evaluate()`, no IR node gains a
field, and no artifact byte moves.
