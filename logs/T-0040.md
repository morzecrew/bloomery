## Plan

P2 asks for `requires_evidence: locked | assumed` on a mart, a classification
pass over the facts its measures rest on, and one aggregated refusal naming the
consumer, the measure, the fact and how the fact was obtained.

Reading the tree before writing any of it turned up a premise the document
rests on that the compiler does not satisfy: **nothing on the compile path
produces a fact that grades below `LOCKED`**, so the refusal has nothing to
refuse and §6's central test cannot be written. Halted on D4. No code written.

## Halt

Halted on **D4** (`LOCKED`). Nothing on the compile path produces a semantic fact
that grades below `LOCKED`, so `requires_evidence: locked` cannot refuse anything
and §6's "a derived fact does not satisfy `locked`" cannot be written as a test.
The three ways out are in the first entry above; all three change the RFC rather
than the code, which is the author's call and not this executor's.

## Self-audit — 2026-09-10

Scope: the whole branch against `main` — 3 commits, 14 files, +710/-8, merge base
`8eb03a1`. Every pass ran. The sabotage sweep is the one that paid: ten mutants over
the new guard, and **five survived the first pass**, which is the worst ratio this
branch has produced and is a direct consequence of the vacuity recorded above — a
corpus in which nothing grades below `LOCKED` cannot exercise most of what the guard
decides. All five are fixed; the second sweep killed all ten.

1. **The rule the corpus cannot see.** `weak_bases` acquits a column that has one
   fully-`LOCKED` route even when another route is weak. Turning that `any` into an
   `all` — a different rule, not a stricter one — killed nothing, because no column in
   any fixture is reached two ways. Extracted from the walk into a public helper and
   asked directly, with both orderings and the mixed-route case. An author who declared
   a relationship must not be refused because the compiler could also have got there
   through a key, and that is now a test rather than a docstring.
2. **`assumed` was skipped by a guard nothing proved was reading it.** Deleting the
   `requires_evidence != _STRICT` skip survived: with every basis `LOCKED`, walking every
   mart finds the same nothing as walking none. The test now monkeypatches the pre-regrade
   mapping first, where a lenient mart returns empty and the same project asking `locked`
   does not — so the emptiness is the requirement being honoured rather than a dead walk.
3. **A refusal repeated once per measure.** The message was emitted per (measure, column),
   so a three-measure mart printed the same sentence three times about one column. It is
   one leaf per column now, naming the measures it carries. `test_every_violation_is_collected`
   had asserted one-leaf-per-column and passed only because the fixture has a single measure.
4. **The absent-mart guard was never entered.** No fixture has a mart that both fails to
   flatten and asks for `locked`, so deleting the branch killed nothing. Tested against a
   draft the mart is genuinely missing from, with a control asserting the same draft *does*
   refuse when the mart is present.
5. **Two over-built branches with no case behind them.** A `min()` over routes picked the
   cheapest one to report, and a `set()` de-duplicated a list that cannot contain a
   duplicate. Neither could be observed; both are gone. The reporting rule is now "name
   every weak basis of every route", which is what a reader needs and what a test can pin.

**Verification.** `just test` 4488 passed / 5 skipped before the audit fixes; `just
quality -s` 9 of 9; ten hand-written mutants over the guard, the spec default, the stage
wiring and the regraded table, all killed on the second sweep. Coverage and patch
coverage are in the commit that follows this entry.

**Residue.** The thing this audit cannot fix is the one the halt already recorded: no
project can trip the refusal, so every test of it restores the pre-regrade mapping first.
That makes the suite a test of the *rule* and not of any project's compliance, and it is
the reason five mutants survived a corpus-driven suite. It is honest and it is thin, and
it stays thin until RFC 0044 P2 gives `IMPORTED_VERIFIED` a producer.
