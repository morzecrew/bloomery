## Why D4 is superseded rather than executed

`logs/T-0050.md` halted on it: D4 refuses `pii`/`secret` on a mapped field whose path is
not redacted, and a mapped field's path *cannot* be redacted — `_check_redaction` refuses
that already — so both branches close and the classification has no legal spelling. §2's
own argument is that a classification must **compose** with `RedactionConflict` rather
than duplicate it, and D4 duplicates it.

The premise expired inside the RFC. §2 pairs classification with `redact:` because
"`redact:` is the nearest thing" — true when 0055 was drafted, and false once phase 4
shipped `grants`, the one annotation in this framework with a mechanism behind it. The
replacement pairs classification with grants.

## Self-audit — 2026-09-13

Scope: `main...HEAD`, 1 commit, 37 files, +925−48 at the start of the sweep. Findings, all
fixed on the branch.

**1. A sabotage survived: one column, two refusals.** Dropping the `continue` after
`SecretPublished` lets a `secret` column whose grants *also* widen collect both refusals,
and every test in the module still passed — the column was refused, just twice. The second
is a second answer to a settled question: fix the classification and the widening refusal
vanishes with it. Pinned as "one column produces at most one classification refusal".

**2. A prose claim needed a test.** The guard's docstring says `MartIR.columns` covers the
columns measures are computed from, not only dimensions. True — `unit_price` feeds
`gross_revenue` and is refused — but it is the claim a reader thinking in Cube terms would
doubt, so it is now a test rather than a sentence.

**3. "Not in the coverage report" was not a gap.** `patch-coverage` reported 21/21 lines
while omitting the 220-line module this branch is mostly *about*, which reads as a hole.
It is `skip_covered = true`: a file at 100% is left out of the report. Measured directly —
49 statements, 14 branches, **100%** — and the `guardrails/` package floor of 100%
(RFC 0009 D9) holds, which is what would have failed first if it did not.

**Sabotage:** 14 mutants over the new surface, 13 killed and the survivor above killed
after the property was pinned; 2 behaviour-preserving controls survived. Two mutations
failed to apply on the first pass — the formatter had rewrapped the lines my patterns
matched — and were re-run rather than counted.

**Boundary cases added:** an entity granting `{select: []}` against a mart granting a role
(both declared, one empty — the case a subset comparison is most likely to get wrong), and
a classified column no relation publishes, which must stay legal because silver is where a
sensitive column is *declared*.

**Checked and clean:** every tier, including the opt-in ones this time —
`just test-all`, 5101 passed. The advisory and the guardrail read one `published_columns()`
walk, so they cannot disagree about which relations are published.

**Residue — what I would still distrust.** The refusal only fires where both sides declare
grants, so on a project that declares none it is the advisory and nothing more; that is the
design, but it means the teeth are proportional to how much access policy lives in the
spec. And `AudienceWidened` compares role *names* as strings — two roles that are the same
group under a warehouse's own hierarchy read as different here, and bloomery has no way to
know otherwise.
