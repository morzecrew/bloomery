## Plan

P1 is the grade vocabulary, its total derivation from `Provenance`, and a rendering
that makes it visible. §12 is explicit that there is **no requirement and no refusal**
here; `requires_evidence` is P2 and exposures are P3, and neither is touched.

**§6 lists seven tests and five of them belong to P2 or P3** — the refusal, the forged
grade, transitivity, aggregation, and the pair that need an annotation to exist. P1's
share is "the default is invisible", which every existing fixture already is, and "every
`Provenance` member has a grade", which §9 names as the one test that must not be
dropped as ritual. A thin-looking test list here is the phase boundary, not a gap.
