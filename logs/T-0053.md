## Deliberately not applied

Two decisions of RFC 0044 were read and are not in question here. D1 (`LOCKED`) constrains
P1 and P1 has landed. D4 (`LOCKED`) — a conflict between an imported and a declared fact
refuses naming both provenances — is sound and is *unimplementable as written* only because
nothing defines when two facts are the same fact across the two layers; that is the fourth
of the five gaps, and it is named in the first entry rather than given an entry of its own,
since it is one clause of the same missing design.

---

## Resolution — 2026-09-13

The author accepted the second option the halt offered: a separate document rather than five
rows onto RFC 0044 §5. It is
[RFC 0070](../rfcs/0070-mechanical-imports-and-per-relationship-provenance.md), and the
three proposals above are its D1, D2 and D4 — cited to their entries there, as an accepted
row must be.

Two things the drafting settled that the halt did not raise, both recorded here because
they changed a document rather than the code:

- **RFC 0044 is now ✅ Complete and stays in the tree**, as the root of a live sequence
  (`INDEX.md`). Its P1 shipped and its P2 moved out, so nothing holds it open — but 0070
  argues from D1–D4 rather than citing them, so retiring it now would leave 0070 reasoning
  from a premise no longer in the tree. It is retired with 0070.
- **bloomery's own MetricFlow output cannot be round-tripped**, which is what a test of the
  importer would reach for first. Compiling `ecom_basic` to MetricFlow produces one
  `semantic_manifest.json` whose single semantic model carries
  `[('order', 'foreign', 'order_id')]` and no model declares `order` as `primary`, because
  emission is per mart. 0070 §3 and §6 are written against a hand-authored manifest for
  that reason.
