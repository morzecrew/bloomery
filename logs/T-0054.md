```divergence
decision: unlisted
grade: UNLISTED
class: spec-gap
at: 2026-09-13T20:12:00Z
attempt: 1
claim: §5.2 says an exposure's requirement applies transitively to "the marts and metrics it names" and §6 says a metric "two hops down" is named in the refusal, but neither says how a metric's facts are reached. A mart has `columns` traced to (source_entity, source_column), which `_weak_columns` walks; a metric has grain, expr and depends_on, and no column tracing. The shared metric-to-mart rule `measure_owners` exists and is public precisely so the emitter and the planner cannot disagree, but it is unreachable from here — `bloomery.emit` sits above `bloomery.guardrails` in the layers contract, so importing it is a contract violation rather than a style question — a lower layer index is a higher layer, and `measure_owners` is defined at src/bloomery/emit/lower/marts.py:233
evidence: `python3 -c "import tomllib,pathlib;l=tomllib.loads(pathlib.Path('pyproject.toml').read_text())['tool']['importlinter']['contracts'][0]['layers'];print('emit',l.index('(bloomery.emit)'),'guardrails',l.index('(bloomery.guardrails)'))"` -> emit 3 guardrails 8
action: decided
proposal: ASSUMED — an exposure's requirement on a metric is checked against **every mart carrying that metric as a measure**, not against the one `measure_owners` would pick. `measure_owners` answers "where is this measure emitted"; the exposure's question is "can the number this dashboard reads rest on something nobody wrote down", and the planner selects a covering mart at request time that need not be the cheapest one. Requiring all of them is the only quantifier under which the exposure's guarantee actually holds. It is deliberately a different quantifier from `weak_bases`, where one fully-`LOCKED` route acquits a column: a route is an alternative *proof* of one fact and the strongest wins, while a mart is a runtime *selection* among relations and the weakest governs
```
