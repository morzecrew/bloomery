GrainMismatch
source: mapping[wms__order_lines->order_item]: fields.landed_cost
derivation of 'landed_cost' combines 'shipping_cost' from entity 'order' (grain: one row per order) at the grain of entity 'order_item' (grain: one row per line on an order), reached via relationship 'item_of_order' (many_to_one) with no aggregation step. Joined across grains, 'shipping_cost' is duplicated once per 'order_item' row and any SUM over the result overstates it. Fix: add an explicit aggregation/allocation over 'order_item', or declare the derivation on entity 'order'
